# Changelog

## 0.4.0

- **Edge glow:** ambient light around your screen edges in your Verse colours.
  Motion: Beat (reacts to your Mac's sound output; macOS 14.2+, asks for System
  Audio Recording once, never the microphone), Lyrics (pulses on every sung word),
  Breathe, or Still. Two-colour balance, thickness, glow strength, choice of
  display, notch-aware, on the desktop and the lock screen.
- **Now-playing card** on the lock screen: cover, title, artist and progress.

## 0.3.1

- **Homebrew:** install with `brew install --cask enrych/tap/verse` (no security prompt) and update with `brew upgrade --cask verse`.
- No changes to the app itself.

## 0.3.0

- **Focus style:** one big line at a time, each word lighting up as it's sung.
- **Album art background:** the song's cover as a softened poster behind the lyrics.
- **Quiet moments:** intros and long breaks preview the next line as faint stars, and the sky gently comes alive.
- **Paused state:** lyrics dim with a small "Paused" badge.
- **Better lyrics coverage:** cleaner song matching, several candidates per song, retries on busy services, plain lyrics as a last resort, and a clear status with Retry.
- **Per-song sync** on top of the global offset.
- **⌃⌥⌘L** toggles the full-screen stage on the display under the pointer.
- **Open at login**, an in-app update notice, and an app icon.
- Clearer **Colours** setting: Warm cream, From album art, or Custom.

## 0.2.0

- **Night background:** near-black with faint dust stars and grain (Black and Wallpaper still available).

## 0.1.0

- First release: lock-screen and full-screen lyrics, Constellation style, Spotify and Apple Music, word-by-word sync.
