WHY THIS EXISTS

I saw a reel of Verci — lyrics drifting across a Mac's lock screen — and loved it. It was paid, and I'm a developer, so I made my own.

Lock your Mac, keep the music on, and the words show up as they're sung. Free and open source.

If you want the polished version, buy Verci. It's lovely.
